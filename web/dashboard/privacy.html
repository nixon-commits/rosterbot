rosterbot Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026

In short. rosterbot is a personal fantasy-sports automation tool. It stores your email address, your fantasy league account credentials, and the roster data it needs to set your lineup on your behalf. It exists to run your team — nothing else. There is no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no profiling, and your data is never sold, rented, or shared for anyone else’s marketing.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the rosterbot iOS app, the rosterbot web dashboard at rosterbot.dev and dash.rosterbot.dev, the public recap site at recaps.rosterbot.dev, and the backend service behind them. rosterbot is operated by an individual, not a company, as a personal project.

rosterbot is invite-only. You cannot create an account without an invite link issued by the operator.

2. What we collect

Account information. Your email address and display name. Your email is entered by the operator when your invite is created and is checked against the address on your Fantrax account when you connect; we record who vouched for it and when. We do not send you email, and there is no mailing list.

Fantasy platform credentials. To set your lineup, rosterbot signs in to your Fantrax account as you. It stores your Fantrax username and password, and the session cookie obtained from them. These are encrypted before storage — see “How credentials are protected” below. It also stores your Fantrax user ID and the ID of the team you manage.

Passkeys. When you register a passkey, rosterbot stores its public key, credential ID, and signature counter. A passkey’s private key never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. rosterbot has no password of yours other than the fantasy-platform credential above.

Fantasy data. Roster contents, lineups set, projections, trade offers and completed trades, game-start budgets, and the historical analytics derived from them. This is data about your fantasy team, retrieved from your league, and it is what the service is for.

Server logs. The public recap site is served through a content delivery network that records standard web access logs, including IP addresses and requested URLs. Those logs are deleted automatically after 90 days. They are used to count how many people read the weekly recap and for nothing else.

What we do not collect. No location data, no contacts, no photos, no health data, no advertising identifiers, no device fingerprints, no browsing history outside this service. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

3. Why we collect it

Every category above is collected for one purpose: operating the service you asked for. Concretely, that means signing in to your league to read your roster, computing an optimal lineup, applying that lineup when you have enabled automatic apply, sending you notifications about lineup changes and trades, and showing you your own performance history in the dashboard.

rosterbot does not use your data to build advertising profiles, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or rent your data to anyone. Nothing here is used for automated decision-making that has legal effects.

4. How credentials are protected

Your Fantrax username, password, and session cookie are encrypted with AWS Key Management Service before being written to storage, under an encryption context scoped to your account — so a ciphertext taken from one user’s record cannot be decrypted as another’s.

The split is enforced in infrastructure, not merely by convention: the public-facing API component is granted permission to encrypt only, and the background job component is granted permission to decrypt only. Neither holds both. A compromise of the public API therefore yields ciphertext without a key.

No security measure is absolute, and rosterbot is a personal project rather than an audited commercial service. You should treat the fantasy-platform credential you give it accordingly, and use a password unique to that account.

5. Who your data is shared with

rosterbot has no advertising partners, no data brokers, and no analytics vendors. It communicates with the following services:

  • Fantrax — your own fantasy account, signed in as you, to read your roster and set your lineup. This is the purpose of the service.
  • Pushover — delivers your notifications. Notification text (for example, a lineup change or a trade alert) passes through it.
  • Amazon Web Services — hosts the service and stores its data.
  • Read-only data sources — MLB Stats API, FanGraphs, Baseball Savant, HittersKeepBatting, Sleeper, and StatsGuy. rosterbot fetches public statistics and projections from these. It sends them no personal information about you.

We may also disclose data where required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights or safety of users of the service.

6. Where your data is stored

On Amazon Web Services infrastructure in the United States (the us-west-1 region). If you use rosterbot from outside the United States, your data is processed there.

7. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can ask the operator to delete your account at any time using the contact address below; deletion is also available to the operator from the dashboard.

Deleting an account removes your profile, your email address, your registered passkeys, and your encrypted fantasy-platform credentials and connection record, and releases your email address and team so they can be used again.

To be precise about what deletion does not remove: historical fantasy analytics files already written to storage — daily projection accuracy records, team-value history, and similar — are retained. After deletion these are orphaned, meaning nothing links them back to your account, your email, or your credentials. If you want these purged as well, say so in your deletion request and they will be removed manually.

Recap-site access logs are deleted automatically after 90 days.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Automatic lineup changes are off by default and are only enabled by your own deliberate action. You can turn them off again at any time in Settings.
  • Disconnect your fantasy account at any time, which stops rosterbot from signing in as you.
  • Revoke a passkey from the dashboard, or sign out every device at once.
  • Access, correct, or delete your data — write to the address below. Because rosterbot has very few users, these requests are handled by hand; expect a reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to a copy of your data and the right to object to processing. rosterbot does not sell personal information as those laws define it. Exercise any of these rights at the address below.

9. Children

rosterbot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The service is invite-only, so accounts are not created without the operator’s involvement. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and it will be removed.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top will be updated and, where the change affects how your data is used, you will be notified through the app before it takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions, data requests, or deletion requests: privacy@rosterbot.dev
