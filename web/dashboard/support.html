rosterbot Support

Help with the rosterbot iOS app

Need help? Write to privacy@rosterbot.dev. rosterbot is a small service run by one person, so a reply can take a few days. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what you saw on screen. Never send your Fantrax password by email.

What rosterbot does

rosterbot reads your Fantrax fantasy baseball roster, works out the best lineup for the day, and can set that lineup for you. It also shows waiver and trade information for your team, and it can show your Sleeper football matchup in a Home Screen widget.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 27 or later.
  • A Fantrax account that owns a team in a league rosterbot supports. If rosterbot cannot find your team after you connect, write to the address above.

Signing in

rosterbot uses a passkey. There is no password to remember. To start, open the app and choose Create Account, or open an invite link if the operator sent you one. Your iPhone then asks you to save a passkey.

Your passkey is kept in your Passwords app and, if you use iCloud Keychain, on your other Apple devices. If you lose every device that has your passkey, write to the address above and ask for a recovery link.

Connecting Fantrax

After you create your passkey, the app asks for your Fantrax sign-in so that rosterbot can read and set your lineup. The check can take a minute or two.

  • Two-factor authentication. rosterbot cannot connect to a Fantrax account that uses two-factor authentication.
  • You changed your Fantrax password. Open Settings in the app and connect Fantrax again with the new password.
  • The check did not finish. Wait a minute and try again. Your password is probably not the problem.

Lineup changes

Automatic lineup changes are off until you turn them on. You can turn Auto-apply lineups on or off at any time in Settings. When it is off, rosterbot only shows you the lineup it recommends.

Notifications

The app asks for permission to send notifications after you sign in. If you said no and want them now, open the iOS Settings app, find rosterbot, and allow notifications.

Sleeper leagues and the widget

You can add your Sleeper leagues in Settings. rosterbot can read Sleeper leagues but never changes them. After you add a league, add the rosterbot widget from the Home Screen widget gallery.

Deleting your account

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. This is permanent. The privacy policy says what is removed and what is not.
