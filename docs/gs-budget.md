# GS Budget: the weekly game-start gate, forecast, and floor alert

Split out of `CLAUDE.md` (2026-09-07) to keep the root file under its char budget. Internal implementation detail for weekly game-start limit awareness — read before touching
`internal/optimizer/gs_budget.go`, `internal/lineuprun/gsbudget.go`, `internal/lineuprun/gs_floor.go`,
`internal/fantrax`'s GS walk, or any GS constant: every number below is measured, not chosen.

**GS budget** — weekly game-start limit awareness (`GS_TRACKING_ENABLED` env var, false = disabled). When enabled, the pitcher optimizer resolves today's scoring period and fetches its real GS max via `GetGSLimits`, gating SP starts to avoid exhausting the weekly GS allocation on low-value starters while better aces pitch later in the matchup week. A live-fetch error sends a Pushover alert (`PUSHOVER_USER_KEY`) and disables the gate for that run rather than falling back to a stale static number; no configured max just disables the gate silently (nothing to track).

**The gate is two-sided, and the two bounds fail differently** (rosterbot-dpm). `GetGSLimits` always returned both, and the call site discarded the minimum with `_`, so the floor was not representable downstream even in principle — the only component that knew it existed was `gs-check`, which reports it the day *after* the period closes, when the penalty is already incurred. `GSBudget.Floor` now carries it and `NeedForFloor()` is the floor-side counterpart to `Remaining()`. A missing **maximum** still disables the gate (nothing to gate against); a missing **minimum** is an ordinary league configuration and must not, so zero is a value here rather than a failure.

**Protection cannot create a start, so the floor also gets an ALERT while the week is still open** (`internal/lineuprun/gs_floor.go`, rosterbot-cs9p). `gs-check` raises `ViolationMin` off `FindJustEndedPeriod` — strictly the morning after the period closed, when the penalty is already taken — and protection inside the gate only stops the ceiling side making a short week worse. The one lever that actually closes a day is a roster claim, and a claim needs lead time. `reportGSFloor` therefore fires from the hourly `optimize` run, reading the `*GSBudget` `ComputeGSBudget` just built and fetching nothing of its own.

Three properties carry it. **Supply is credited from TOMORROW onward, never from today**: today's starts enter `GSBudget.Used` the moment Fantrax settles them and nothing at this seam separates a settled start from a pending one — the exact ambiguity that made the old live-roster count diverge every evening (rosterbot-cg8l). `GSBudget.TodayUnsettled` (rosterbot-ogtq) now credits today's confirmed-but-unlocked probables against `Need` inside `evaluateGSFloor` for exactly that seam — restricted to pitchers actually occupying an active P slot and capped at the slot count, since Used only ever reflects active-slot deltas — so only the estimated-for-today subset and the brief locked-but-not-yet-settled window remain uncredited. **The firing window is bounded at BOTH ends, and the upper bound is now swept rather than reasoned from one week.** `gsFloorMinDaysLeft` (2) keeps the alert actionable, since an alert on the final evening names a problem no claim can still fix — a value chosen on that actionability argument alone until rosterbot-1tia's follow-up swept it too, against the same 150-team-week corpus at the shipped credit=1.0/max=3 pair: under-floor recall reads 24/25 weeks at minDaysLeft=1 (excluded anyway — one day is not enough to clear a waiver claim and then start the pitcher), 19/25 at 2 (shipped), and 17/25 at 3 under the realised-credited reading (22/25 vs 21/25 zero-credited), so 3 loses recall against 2 on both readings and is not adopted — by two weeks and one week, a margin thin enough that only the ranking is the finding. 2 stays, now for a measured reason (see `gsFloorMinDaysLeft`'s comment in `gs_floor.go` for the full table). `gsFloorMaxDaysLeft` (**3**) keeps it out of the early week. It exists because the first version fired on a healthy one — excluding today is right, and before `TodayUnsettled` it left the projection missing exactly today's starts, worst at week start where `Need` was simultaneously maximal because `Used` was still zero; measured on the real Period 21 snapshots, Monday read confirmed 3 + estimated 7.4 → 8.92 credited against a Need of 10, on the week the bead designates as must-not-fire. It is **3** because a 150-team-week replay of the shipped rule (`TestDiagGSFloorSweep`, the 15 Mondays 2026-03-30..2026-07-06 across ten teams, a week finishing under the 10-start floor as the positive) reads max 3 → 45 alerts / 19 TP / P 0.422 / R 0.760, max 4 → 51 / 20 / 0.392 / 0.800, max 5 → 57 / 22 / 0.386 / 0.880: all three clear the flat baseline's recall of 0.760 and 3 carries the best precision. **That Monday range is not the cache's span** (2026-03-25..08-16): the weeks `diagWeeks` drops are the late-season ones — 07-13 and 08-10 lose the week itself, 07-20/07-27/08-03 lose their trailing window to the All-Star-break gap — so the sweep is fitted on the season's first fifteen weeks, where fewer pitchers have cleared `gsStartRateMinOpportunities` and the estimator sits closer to the flat fallback. **That value is sensitive to the replay sample**, which is worth knowing before re-running it: on an intermediate harness that normalized the schedule and clamped the window but still admitted the 30 weeks whose 28-day window straddles the cache's All-Star-break gap — weeks where the estimator silently computes the flat answer under a "weighted" label — the same sweep selects 4. Check the "priced NOBODY" line in `TestDiagStartRateWeeklyCalibration` before trusting any table under it. **The projection itself is not wrong**: `Used` + undiscounted supply read 13.40 on both Monday and Tuesday of Period 21, matching that audit's own ~13.4. Only its split into banked and still-to-come is uninformative that early, so the guard refuses to evaluate rather than reweighting anything — the choice `periodIsVolatile` makes for a period still settling. Inside the window the rule needs no urgency coefficient: as probables are announced the estimate collapses into confirmations and a day on which none of our arms was named stops contributing at all. That same collapse is what makes the empty days *nameable* — a day cannot be known empty in advance (on both zero-start days of period 20 our clubs played and other pitchers started), so the alert becomes possible at exactly the moment it becomes specific. The cost is deliberate: a week already doomed on Monday is not announced until Thursday, because a Monday alert that is wrong most weeks mutes the channel.

**`gsFloorEstimateCredit` is 1.0 — no discount — and that is now MEASURED, not a prior.** It was 0.8, bracketed by hand against the only two weeks with recorded shape (Period 21 finished ~+3 clear and must not fire; Period 20 finished exactly ON its floor and must fire), at a time when `buildGSForecast` priced every unannounced SP at a flat 1-in-5 — an estimate measured to over-forecast by +4.56 starts a week. The credit was a hand-rolled correction for that bias, and the estimator now carries its own (bias +0.76), so applying both discounts the same over-confidence twice. Replayed over 150 team-weeks (every week whose own days **and** whose trailing rate window are fully cached): keeping 0.8 alongside the corrected estimator fires on **87 of 150 weeks at precision 0.253** against a 16.7% base rate, where credit 1.0 fires on 45 at precision 0.422 and exactly the flat baseline's recall (0.760, baseline 43/0.442). **Every one of those figures is a LOWER BOUND on production's true alert rate, not a point** — the replay credits "today" with the day's REALISED starts (hindsight), while production's `GSBudget.TodayUnsettled` credits only confirmed-and-unlocked probables and collapses to 0 the moment those games lock, which is most of the day; realised is always `>=` that subset, so the replay fires less often than production would on the same week. Crediting today with 0 instead — production's own worst case — the same sweep reads **97/150 at precision 0.227** for credit 1.0 and **129/150 at precision 0.194** for credit 0.8, and the ranking is unchanged under both extremes: credit 1.0 / max 3 is still best. See `TestDiagGSFloorSweep`, which now prints both readings for exactly this reason. **No weighted setting beats the flat baseline's precision, and the comment now says so** — an earlier version claimed superiority on both readings, and that claim came from a harness that re-derived the estimator's inputs (see below). Scoring "at or under the floor" instead — Period 20's own case, since a week landing exactly on the floor is not a violation under the strict reading — the chosen rule reads 0.644/0.630 against the baseline's 0.674/0.630: the same recall at slightly lower precision. On this alert the two are a wash; the corrected estimator is carried for the **gate**, where it moves 35.9% of decisions. Confirmed starts are deliberately **not** discounted, and at 1.0 nothing else is either, which makes the constant a no-op multiplier kept for what it documents. **rosterbot-1tia's premise has moved**: it assumed the sweep needed `gs_floor`/`gs_forecast` in the projection snapshots (first gradeable week 2026-08-31), but the frozen per-day roster snapshots replay the whole season without them. The two designated weeks themselves are NOT replayable — the cached MLB schedule stops at 2026-08-16 and the pitcher-GS snapshots at daily period 152 — so they survive as fixtures in `TestEvaluateGSFloor_PinsTheSweptFloorConstants`.

Dedup is one marker per **(season, weekly period)** under `layout.GSFloorAlerts` (`alerts/gs-floor/`, PerTenant like `ILStarts` — the minimum is a per-team obligation, so two short tenants are two problems). Order is **check → send → mark**, a marker failure degrades to a duplicate and never to silence, `--dry-run` skips both, and nil markers alert every run rather than going quiet. The season is in the key because weekly period numbers restart each year (the correction rosterbot-qoa made to the past-period cache keys). The `gs floor check:` coverage line prints **unconditionally**, including on a comfortable week, for the same reason as `il-start check:` — this check's normal output is silence, so without the inputs a healthy week and a structurally blind trigger read identically, which is how the forecast reported 0.0 all season (rosterbot-1jvj).

**Promotion, as the bead originally specified it, is not implementable, and that is a measurement not an opinion.** MLB names the probables, so nothing the optimizer does can manufacture a start: on both zero-start days of period 20 our clubs played and *other* pitchers took the ball. The only real lever is refusing to spend the ceiling gate against a week already short, so `applyGSGate` computes a `floorReserve` and tops `keepToday` back up in the same value order the cut used, reporting the rescued starts as `GSGateReport.Protected`. Two properties are load-bearing. **`floorReserve` subtracts CONFIRMED future starts only, never `Estimated`** — staking the floor on rotation math's ~1-in-5 claim over a club that has named nobody is precisely how a floor-bound week ends up under the floor, and weekly GS is use-it-or-lose-it so the unspent probability is burned rather than banked. And **the clamp to `remaining` makes the ceiling constraint a consequence rather than an assertion**: the cut placed at most `remaining` entries, the top-up raises the count to at most `floorReserve ≤ remaining`, so protection provably cannot push the week past `Limit`. Note the practical consequence for a roster that habitually finishes at the minimum: the floor dominates the ceiling for most of most weeks and the gate will rarely suppress, which is the correct outcome for such a roster and self-corrects as `Used` climbs and `NeedForFloor()` falls to zero.
- **Matchup week boundaries** derived from `GetAllMatchups()`: consecutive daily scoring periods where the team faces the same opponent form a matchup week. Computed in `fantrax/matchup_weeks.go` via `MatchupWeekBounds`.
- **Used-GS counting walks Fantrax's own per-day snapshots, today included** (rosterbot-cg8l). `ComputeGSBudget` gets the whole figure from one `GetTeamGS` call over `weekStart..today` — the same active-slot YTD-delta walk `gs-check` uses league-wide — so a start is counted from that day's frozen roster state, not from the roster as it looks now. Today used to be counted separately by `countTodayStarts`, off the **live** roster (active + team locked + today's probable). That answers "is he active NOW" when the question is "did he start TODAY", and the two diverge every evening: once a pitcher's game ends a later hourly run rotates him back to Reserve for tomorrow and his already-spent start silently drops out. Production on 2026-08-19 read 1/12 through the afternoon, peaked at the correct 5/12 at 00:00Z, then fell to 3/12 an hour later and stayed there; the next morning's walk over the same days reported 5. Measured live against Fantrax, the walk recovers exactly the four Wednesday starts the live-roster count lost (`DIAG_GS_TODAY=2026-08-19 DIAG_GS_WEEK_START=2026-08-17 go test -tags diag -run TestDiagGSWalkIncludesToday ./internal/fantrax/`). Two consequences: **`gsPeriodWalk`/`GetTeamGS` now cap the walk at today rather than yesterday**, with the caller's `sp.EndDate` deciding how far to go — `gs-check` grades a period that closed yesterday, so the cap never binds there — and a period that **starts today** now walks that day instead of returning nil, which is what makes a start on the week's opening day visible before the following morning. It also removed a silent failure mode: a statsapi outage used to zero today's contribution to `Used` (the old count needed `LockedTeams` **and** `ProbableStarters`), where the walk does not consult the schedule at all. The error direction was over-permissive — the gate believed 9 starts remained when 7 did — benign at the GS floor and inverting against the max. This only became load-bearing once rosterbot-1jvj revived the forecast; before that the gate never suppressed anything, so a wrong used-count could not bite.
- **Future demand forecasting is hybrid PER CLUB, not per day** (rosterbot-1jvj). Each rostered SP lands in exactly one bucket for a given future day: named as its club's probable → a confirmed start counted exactly; club plays but has named nobody yet → rotation-math demand; club plays and named someone else → nothing, the day is settled for us; club idle → nothing. **The rotation-math bucket is weighted per pitcher, not divided by 5** (rosterbot-goht): each unannounced SP contributes his own trailing-28-day active-slot start rate (`computeStartRates`, shrunk toward 1/5 by `gsStartRatePrior` = 2 pseudo-opportunities; no history → exactly 1/5, bit-identical to the old `SPs/5`). The flat divisor was not merely noisy but biased — over 169 pitcher-seasons the measured rate is mean 0.129 / sd 0.057 / p90 0.191 / max 0.216, so 1/5 sits above 94.1% of them, and forecasting each of 150 team-weeks from its Monday it predicted 15.61 starts against 11.05 realised (bias +4.56, MAE 4.58) where the weighting predicts 11.82 (bias +0.76, MAE 1.72). The rate is **active-slot conversion**, which is exactly what `Used` spends — the StatsType=2 snapshot's GS advances only for a captured active-slot start — and the history read is **soft** (a failure reverts everyone to 1/5 and logs `start-rate history unavailable`), unlike the fatal schedule seam, because its failure has one known direction: the behaviour that shipped all season.

  **Three things make the tally trustworthy, and each was a defect first.** The denominator counts only days the pitcher was *available*: his club played, he was SP-eligible, and he was neither on the IL nor in the minors (`fantrax.StatusIL`/`StatusMinors`, joined from that period's roster-stats snapshot by `GetTeamPitcherDaysWithStatus` — the same snapshot `DailyFantasyPoints` already walks, so no new upstream). Without that, 7.5% of consulted pitcher-days carried more than two unavailable days and Gerrit Cole read 0.086 over 26 "opportunities" of which 24 were days he could not have pitched for us, against 0.600 over the two that were real. A **first-appearance row is dropped whole** (`PitcherDay.FirstAppearance`): its capped delta can credit a start belonging to another team's season, and a genuine start on such a day is indistinguishable from a bench day. And `gsStartRateMinOpportunities` (**5**, one rotation turn) plus `gsStartRateCap` (**0.25**, one turn in four club-games) refuse to price a pitcher on less history than a turn and bound what survives — without them 104 of 2255 prices exceeded any rate a rotation can produce, a pitcher seen once reading 0.467. Neither is chosen by the sweep and neither pretends to be: the guard **costs** 0.11 of whole-week MAE (1.61 at minOpps 0 against 1.72 at 5) and is taken on the physical argument plus the 78 impossible prices it deletes. The cap is 0.25 rather than 1/RotationSize because the denominator is club-game-days and three pitchers with full-season samples genuinely read above 0.20 (Civale 0.216, Cole 0.215, Perez 0.214); a 0.20 cap does read better (bias +0.60 / MAE 1.64 against +0.76 / 1.72) and is rejected anyway, because it buys that by clipping 227 of 1954 prices — every one a pitcher measured above one turn in five, i.e. exactly the arms the gate is deciding about.

  **The estimator the harness measures IS the one production runs** — `tallyStartRates` is the pure kernel both call, over rows the shipped `fantrax.PitcherDayWalk` derived. That was the original defect: rosterbot-goht's harness applied filters production did not, and on the same pitchers the two read n=160 mean 0.129 p90 0.192 against n=225 mean 0.081 p90 0.178. **That guarantee reaches the INPUTS too, and that is where it broke a second time**: the harness rebuilt `computeStartRates`' inputs by hand and got three things wrong — a schedule map built from raw statsapi abbreviations where production normalizes through `internal/teams` (so ARI and CHW pitcher-days never matched: 880 of them), a trailing window with no clamp at the season opener (so 40 weeks measured all-flat), and, since `pitcherDays` refuses a partially-cached window by returning an EMPTY rate table that every caller reads as "price everyone flat", 30 more weeks straddling the cache's All-Star-break schedule gap that computed the flat baseline and were reported as the weighted estimator. Every published figure moved. If a value in the harness has a production spelling, it must use the production spelling; and a week the estimator cannot be evaluated on must be excluded, not scored. **The flip percentages are an UPPER BOUND, not production**: the replay runs in the open announcement regime (nobody has named anyone), the only regime the weighting can move at all, and reads 35.9% of gate (323) and 5.2% of floor (47) decisions over 900 in-week points; MLB trickles probables out days ahead (rosterbot-1jvj) and the closed regime is identical between flat and weighted by construction, so the real effect lies between zero and those. `internal/lineuprun/diag_startrate_test.go` (tag `diag`, `DIAG_CACHE_DIR=`) is the harness for every number above. It costs 57 snapshot reads (29 pitcher-GS, 28 roster-stats — the baseline day reads no roster snapshot) and 28 schedule reads per `ComputeGSBudget`, all cached; `shadow` shares one walk across its four passes via `lineuprun.NewStartRateCache`, which is **keyed on nothing**, so a caller must only share one across Runs with identical team, date and window. The `GS start rates:` coverage line and its soft-fail WARNING ride `GSDecision.Notices`, which `Run` writes to `Options.Out` on every run — not `GSDecision.Logs`, which only `--verbose` ever shows; the first production run after the weighting deployed printed `gs floor check:` and not this line, because it had been routed through `Logs`. **The slot cap applies to starts, i.e. after the division, not to the pitcher count before it** — capping first mixes units and understates every day where more rostered SPs have a game than there are pitcher slots (rosterbot-abd) — and it bounds the day's **combined** confirmed + estimated total, since confirmed starts have already claimed slots the estimate may not re-spend.
- **The grain is the whole correctness story, and getting it wrong made the fallback dead code for the entire season.** Until rosterbot-1jvj the two regimes were selected per DAY on `len(probs) > 0` — the size of the **league-wide** probables map. MLB trickles probables out up to a week ahead, so that map is essentially never empty on a future day: measured against statsapi on 2026-08-19, every remaining day of the matchup week already carried some announced probables against 18–30 clubs playing. **A single announcement for any club, ours or not, zeroed the entire day**, and the estimate branch only ever ran on a day the league had announced literally nothing — measured end to end on the real roster, `FutureDemand()` read **0.00** where the per-club fix reads **5.80**. The damage was not confined to the display: `applyGSGate` ranks today's starters against today + future demand, so with future demand structurally zero it saw no competition for the remaining budget and **never suppressed anything** — which is the mechanism behind the chronic `0 start(s) suppressed … 0.0 gross projected pts` line that appears in every backtest and had been read as normal. Which clubs have announced is derived from the probables map's **values** (a club appears there iff one of its games has a non-null `probablePitcher`), which is per-club information from the same payload; `len(probs)` is a league-wide count and says nothing about any particular club.
- **`FutureDemand()` and `applyGSGate` must both SUM a day's two regimes, never prefer one.** `ConfirmedStarters` covers the clubs that have named a starter and `Estimated` covers the clubs that have not, so they describe **disjoint** sets of our pitchers; the old either/or read discarded the estimate on every partially-announced day, which is most of them. The two accumulations sit in different packages and must stay in step — the display reports `FutureDemand()` while the gate re-derives the same total inline.
- **A schedule-seam failure is FATAL to the gate, not a degraded forecast.** `buildGSForecast` returns an error; both `TeamsPlayingOn` and `ProbableStarters` used to discard theirs, which made an upstream outage indistinguishable from a genuinely quiet week. The tempting middle ground — keep the partial forecast, since an under-count only makes the gate suppress *less* — does not survive the probables case: losing that map drops confirmed starts (worth 1.0 each) while promoting every already-settled club into the estimate (worth 0.2 each), so the error runs in an **unknown direction**. `ComputeGSBudget`'s standing rule exists precisely so nobody adjudicates that per failure mode: a wrong budget silently mis-shapes pitcher usage for the rest of the week, and no budget does not. The forecast is simply the last step of the same cascade. It raises **no Pushover**, unlike the GS-limit fetch — a statsapi blip is transient and self-heals on the next hourly run, where a Fantrax config read that stops working does not. Cost is bounded by cadence rather than by severity: one blip costs one run's gate.
- **The loop aborts on the first unreadable day** (`TestBuildGSForecast_StopsAtTheFirstUnreadableDay`): the week's total is already disqualified, so the remaining days teach nothing and there is no reason to spend more round-trips on a seam that just failed.
- **This is safe only because a gameless day is not an error.** Verified live: statsapi returns HTTP 200 with an empty `dates` array for an off-day, so `TeamsPlayingOn` yields an empty map and a nil error. Were absence encoded as an error, making the seam fatal would disable the gate on every off-day and across the All-Star break — the exact window where period math is already hardest.
- The live harness that reproduced and verified this is `internal/lineuprun/diag_gsforecast_test.go` (build-tagged `diag`, `DIAG_SP_TEAMS=... DIAG_WEEK_END=... go test -tags diag -run TestDiagGSForecast`), which takes the roster as club abbreviations so it needs no credentials or chromedp login.
- **Value-ranked gate** (`optimizer/gs_budget.go`, `applyGSGate`): when remaining budget can't cover every planned start (today + future confirmed + future estimated), pools all of them into one ranking by expected points and keeps only the top `remaining` by value — it does not split the budget proportionally by day/count. Future days without announced probables contribute `ceil(Estimated)` placeholder entries valued at the roster-wide mean SP projection (a real projection, not a scrub), **priced as a marginal-value ladder**: the k-th such entry is worth `placeholder × min(max(Estimated-(k-1), 0), 1)`, i.e. full value while whole units of expected demand remain and the leftover fraction after that. So `Estimated = 1.4` emits one entry at full placeholder and one at 0.4× it. **The weight belongs in the price precisely because the cut spends a whole slot** — the gate's question is "hold this slot or spend it today?", and holding is worth `placeholder × min(E,1)` in expectation against a certain value today. This reads like a units error (every other entry is points-per-start; this one is points × probability) and was filed as one, but pricing the tail at full value would bench a certain start to hold a slot for a merely-likely future one — and since weekly GS is use-it-or-lose-it, the unspent probability is burned, not banked (**rosterbot-idn**, closed as not-a-bug; `gs_budget_test.go` pins the ladder). Ties prefer today's starters (a certain start) over future ones, then break on player ID. **`certaintyMargin = 0.10`** extends that tie preference to near-ties: an *estimated* future entry must beat today's value by more than 10% to outrank it (confirmed future starts are as certain as today's and are excluded). It prices `P(the estimated start never materializes)`, **not** projection error — SP projection MAE is large but symmetric, and haircutting an unbiased estimate biases the ranking rather than de-risking it. The 0.10 itself is an unmeasured prior, not a fitted value — and it **cannot be swept from archived data**, because `DayForecast` was never persisted (the GS walk records what happened, not what was believed, and the probables cache merges in place rather than keeping a time series). `Snapshot.GSForecast` now archives the gate's forward view per day so the sweep becomes possible going forward; it can only be accumulated, never backfilled, and any pre-2026-08-19 value would read ≈0 anyway since the rotation-math branch was unreachable until rosterbot-1jvj. Starters that don't make the cut have `IsStarter` flipped to false, applying the existing `NonStarterSPDiscount` (0.05x). Uses `eps = 1e-9` for float comparison consistency. Locked players are exempt from suppression (their GS is already decided one way or the other).
- The gate only applies to today's optimization (the daily GHA run). Future dates in `--dates` ranges are optimized without the gate since each day gets its own run.
- The `--matchup` flag on the optimize command resolves to all remaining days in the current matchup period (from today through the matchup week end).
